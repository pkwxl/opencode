# Survey

Gate: human
Phase-artifacts: survey.md

## plan duties

Survey duties — this phase sizes the reference and hands the scope forks to the person.
Inventory-level reading only: no distillation, no specification (the spec-read phase
after it does that), and no implementation. Size first, and let the size set the
posture: enumerate the reference at the location the brief names — subsystem areas,
line counts, structure, the platform interfaces at the boundary, the wall kind the
brief declares — in this planning session's own read-only survey. A reference of a
few hundred to ~1k lines in one or two areas with no open scope decisions is exactly
one task: read it, write the short survey, and record `Forks: none` — the round then
flows straight into the deep phase. A reference of tens of thousands of lines, many
areas, or genuine MUST/depth/platform forks gets the full posture: the inventory
tasks this phase needs (usually one — inventory is enumeration, not reading) plus a
closing consolidation task that alone writes survey.md in this phase directory, made
of: the reference's map (subsystem areas, sizes, structure), each scope fork as a
`Fork:` line with its options, consequences and a recommended default, the
depth/grading questions, environment facts and gaps (a missing tool is a gap, named
as one — it becomes a needs-attention item of the round report), a proposed MUST
baseline, and beside that evidence a `## Project brief` section proposing the
project's constants: the goal in the target's own terms, the reference location and
wall kind (under rule separation, the platform boundary), the target, the
constraints that bind every round, and the scope posture with each fork's
recommended default. The survey proposes, the person disposes — never decide a fork,
and never write the person's `Clarified:` line.
