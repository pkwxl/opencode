## The ruling

### D1 — The claim address: the phase's own qualified id

Protocol claims are unit-addressed; the boundary's distillation has no
task — ruled: **the address is the phase's own qualified id** (`claim
{"task": "R-01.P01", "kind": "distill"}`). Grounds: the durable record is
*phase-shaped* here — the phase's state files
(`docs/R-NN/P<nn>-<type>/todo.md`/`done.md`), the `docs/R-NN/phases.md`
row and tick, and the handover document itself; phases **are** units in
the document/unit machinery (`parseIndex(text, "phase")`,
`unitStatePaths(phaseRef(unit))`, `scanUnitStates` — the same unit state
protocol the tasks run), so the coordinator's per-unit machinery
(`latest`, `attempts`, receipts, leases) keys on the phase id without a
second address space (P5/P6). 0276 declined the phase id for formulation
because *that* durable record was task-shaped (the index row, the todo,
the done rename, the tick) — the grounds are met by their opposite here,
and the declined alternative (forking `latest(task)` and the close-out's
unit machinery) would apply to this kind if the record were task-shaped;
it is not. The write surface rides the grant as data — `handover`, the
document path (`phaseHandoverDoc`'s own construction), derived once at
preview, read by the fence and the collect, never looked up twice; one
additive wire field (the least the arm needs — 0276's two-field
discipline, one field here because the unit *is* the phase and no
`index` anchor exists: the boundary session writes no index).

### D2 — The lease shape: serial, the main tree

The boundary declares no `Touches:` (it is not a task), so no lane tier
admits it — and a handover-route phase has no ready tasks, so
`scopeCandidates` is already empty by its own predicate: **no gate added**
(the formulate slice needed one because the formulation unit is itself a
pending task; here the standing emptiness is the gate). The distill
grant rides the serial shape: no scope, the main checkout, the serial
reservation holding every other claimant off; a landed distill receipt
is terminal (D6) and releases the reservation.

### D3 — The claimable derivation, the brief, and the boundary's own route

The distill step derives at the one home the push dispatch's predicates
own: `deriveExecutorPreview`'s boundary tail — the handover route (the
index present, tasks listed, every one done — `routePhase`'s own
statuses) MINUS the push skip condition (`validHandover` of the phase's
handover document — the idempotent skip that goes straight to
archiving), held behind the person's human gate (the standing
survey-release admission arm, plus the `clarification:` filter covering
the missing-survey shape — the same "human gate ahead of any session"
discipline `transferAdvance` itself runs). The snapshot reads the two
durable inputs once beside the standing ones (the following phase, the
handover's validity), so the pure derivation never touches disk. The
targeted-claim wait lets the phase's own qualified id through to the
boundary derivation — every other target keeps the standing not-ready
wait — and a failed, expired or released distill lease routes the phase
id as the execute continuation routes its task, the retained partial
handover (the family's own write) riding the next attempt instead of the
clean-tree block. The brief is the same derivation the push-side
distillation session reads: `renderPhaseHandover` over the phase entry,
the handover path, the next phase's title (undefined for the round's
last phase — the template's own last-phase arm), and the closed-task
listing; the acceptance-draft arm never renders (executor mode refuses
the gate). `MAX_RESPONSE_BYTES` applies through the standing
`withAnswers` bound. The floor: a phase with pending work, an open
round, a complete round, or a valid handover already on disk is
byte-identical (P10, pinned) — the absent-index wait, the execute rows,
the open-round wait, the phase-boundary step.

### D4 — The landing fence's per-kind permitted arm: the handover document alone

The distill arm admits **exactly the granted handover document** (the
grant's `handover` anchor), placed after the standing refusal so the
never-set keeps everything else refused: `phases.md` (the tick is the
apply's), the phase's own `todo.md`/`done.md` (the completion rename is
the apply's — the person's transfer through `completePhase`), another
phase's handover document, `subtasks.md`, the workdir, config,
`AGENTS.md`. The early return keeps the phase-unit id out of
`taskDocPaths` entirely (the task-doc arms are task-unit-shaped). The
fence admits the write surface; the settlement judges the grammar (P7).
The distill kind takes **no freeform floor and no handoff arm**: the
push distillation is a one-shot bypass session with no Status-line
channel — the deliverable is a document, and a continuation rides a new
attempt (the push reset-and-rewrite shape), never a handoff parse.

### D5 — The settlement: the mechanical collect, and the push machinery not rebuilt

The distill settlement runs the push collect's own grammars, ported to
the settlement's home from the document layer's own functions —
`validHandover` (the four mandatory sections, `HANDOVER_SECTIONS`' own
predicate) and `constraintPitfallResolves` (the OPEN-status lint, 0082
§10 D13 — an AUTO-RESOLVE marker under "Constraints and pitfalls" is
status laundering). Read-only, ahead of any staging and past the
zero-write answer (a share that wrote nothing has no output to judge —
the formulate precedent's zero-write floor), replayable; its problems
are the push collect's own vocabulary, the holder's remedy a rewrite of
its own document — the plain blocked answer by 0272's classification,
never escalated. Three push halves deliberately not rebuilt (P21 — no
window on the protocol): the acceptance-draft check (executor mode
refuses the gate — the draft cannot exist), the write-time charter
verifier (a default-off experiment-switch session, D3's letter), and
the push reset's `rm(handoverFile)` (the holder is live: the next
attempt rewrites the document wholesale, exactly the push path's own
retry shape). The share commits under the push distillation's own stage
word `phase-handover` — no new stage vocabulary.

### D6 — The verify question: settled, not verified — the landed share terminal, the apply the person's

Ruled explicitly: **no independent verify share for the distill kind;
the settlement judges grammars alone, and the landed share is terminal
for its unit.** Grounds, against P3 and the push path's own shape: the
push path's review seam for the handover is **one-shaped** — the
driver's mechanical collect (grammars, ported to the settlement) plus
the person's own boundary review (the advance's stop, the phase gates at
`completePhase`) — the wrap-up session 0276 converged for formulate (the
executor's verify kind *being* that session) does not exist here: the
distillation IS the boundary's one-shot session, and `renderWrapup` is
task-shaped (no phase-unit brief exists to render — the verify kind
cannot even carry this work). The write-time charter check is a
default-off switch, not a standing fresh-session judgment (0276's own
precedent for push-side session concerns). An independent verify share
would be new judgment machinery with no push twin (P21) and would widen
the boundary's bracket beyond the person's (P2). Therefore: no verify
row ever derives over a distill producer (the verification walk's guard
answers the fresh-claim step), the same-token claim reads the boundary
step — never 0273's settled terminal, that is the verify producer's
shape — and the landed receipt is terminal in `preview`'s closedUnit
predicate and the serial reservation's own (no close-out rides the
coordinator: the completion rename and tick are the apply, the person's
`transfer` through the push path's own idempotent skip — the complete
document on a committed tree, the interrupted-handover shape C.2 already
recovers). The next step after a landed share is the phase-boundary
step, whose standing remedy names the person's act ("Run transfer to
advance the phase.") — the composition teaching itself.
