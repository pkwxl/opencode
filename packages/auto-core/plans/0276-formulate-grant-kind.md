## The ruling

### D1 — The claim address: the formulation unit's own task-shaped id

Protocol claims are task-addressed; formulation is per-phase — ruled: **the
address is the formulation unit's own task id** (`claim {"task": "T-004",
"kind": "formulate"}`). Grounds: the durable record is task-shaped — the
index row, the todo, the done rename, the tick — and the coordinator's
whole machinery (per-unit lookups, attempts, receipts, the close-out) is
unit-addressed; one formulation per phase (formulationState's construction
rule) makes the unit↔phase mapping functional both directions; and no
second address space is invented (P5/P6 — the phase id as task address
would fork `latest(task)` and the close-out's unit machinery, and would be
a new claim vocabulary besides). The phase's identity rides the grant as
data — `phase`, the qualified id, and `index`, the phase's task-index path
(the same field name `VerificationPreview` already carries for the same
concept) — derived once at preview time from the loaded plan, read by the
fence and the collect, never looked up twice.

### D2 — The lease shape: serial, the main tree

The formulation task declares no `Touches:` (construction's own render), so
it is neither documentation-lane eligible (`docEligible` requires a
docs-only declaration) nor worktree-lane eligible (the tier requires a
declared scope) — and the push path's own rule runs it "the serial path by
design (0095 D5: a phase's only ready unit, its Touches everything, ahead
of everything it plans)". The formulate grant rides the serial shape: no
scope, the main checkout, the serial reservation holding every other
claimant off. `scopeCandidates` gains the matching gate — while the phase's
formulation stands, there are no scoped candidates (the formulation is the
phase's only claimable work), which changes nothing for a phase past its
formulation (the gate reads the same `awaitingFormulation` predicate the
dispatch keys on) and makes the formulate step reachable on the menu
beside standing lane work. Declined: the documentation-scoped same-tree
tier and worktree lanes — the declared-concurrency channel (P17) has no
declaration to admit them on.

### D3 — The claimable derivation, the brief, and the follow-up route

The formulate step derives at the one home the push dispatch's predicate
owns: `deriveExecutorPreview`'s awaiting branch — `awaitingFormulation`
(pending or blocked, never in progress — an in_progress unit belongs to a
push session or its recovery ladder and is conservatively waited, P12)
replaces the standing "The phase needs a task plan or formulation
dispatch" wait, which itself remains byte-identical for the absent index
(the construction is the person's `transfer`/`plan` act — the protocol arm
never constructs; the compose-and-verify loop, the synthesis pipeline and
the plan gate stay push-side). The brief is the same derivation the
push-side planning session reads: `renderWhole` over the formulation task,
its todo the single input, the same facts and views — 0217's stage-scoped
role is a session-chain tier concern the protocol's grant does not carry.
`MAX_RESPONSE_BYTES` applies through the standing `withAnswers` bound. The
follow-up route (`readExecutorFollowUp`'s verification and fix rounds over
the landed share's unit) bypasses the awaiting branch — the unit's wrap-up
and fix sessions claim it while its todo still stands pending, exactly the
push path's shape; the bypass rides a `followUp` route flag the fresh-claim
path never sets. Claim's asked-kind guard (new, dead code for the standing
kinds whose routing guarantees the match, load-bearing here) answers a
mismatched targeted ask with the honest wait naming it; an untargeted claim
takes the next step whatever its kind, the menu's row having named it.

### D4 — The landing fence's per-kind permitted arm: the formulation family

The formulation family — the phase's task index (the grant's `index`
anchor), the planned tasks' pending documents (`docs/T-NNN/todo.md`, the
grammar **minus the formulation unit's own todo** — the session's single
input, never its write), and the unit's coverage map (`taskDoc`'s own
construction) — is admitted for grants carrying the formulation context:
formulate grants and fix rounds over the formulation unit (the plan's
repair, the context derived in `readExecutorFollowUp` from the task's own
`Formulate:` field). The arm precedes the standing refusal for exactly
this family; everything the never-set refuses for every kind stays refused
— `done.md` (the todo→done rename is the close-out's alone), `subtasks.md`,
`phases.md`, another phase's index, the workdir, the config directory,
`AGENTS.md`. The fence admits the write surface; the settlement judges the
grammar (P7). The formulate kind takes **no freeform floor**: the planning
session's deliverables are documents, the fence admits exactly them beside
the standing handoff arm (a formulate share may hand over, and continues
like an execute share). A park directive in a formulate handoff meets the
standing unrecognized-handoff-status block — the planning unit has no
dependency-park semantics, its consumables the plan itself.

### D5 — The settlement: the mechanical collect, and the two push checks not rebuilt

The formulate settlement runs the push collect's own grammars, ported to
the settlement's home from the decision layer's own functions — the unit's
own row staying in the index (the driver ticks and closes through it), the
first attempt's fixed prefix (the pre-existing rows and their state files
byte-identical to the claim-time base, `contentAtBase` over the grant's
booked base), `plannedTaskProblems` (the rows' grammar, each new document's
mandatory spec and `Phase:` field, the dependency graph, `takenTaskIds`
and the numbering floor), and the coverage check (`ledgerOf` over the
landed todo, `coverageProblems` against the map). Read-only, before any
staging, replayable; its problems are the push collect's own messages, the
holder's remedy a write inside its own family — the plain blocked answer
by 0272's classification, never escalated. Two push checks have no window
on the protocol and are deliberately not rebuilt (P21): the **landed-todo
digest** (the fence excludes the unit's own todo, so an edit is an
out-of-scope never-set row that `neverSetRows` escalates to the person —
the push digest guarded a commit window the lease's live tree does not
have) and the **driver's own-row restore** (the holder is live: the
blocked voice teaches restoring its own row, a write inside the family —
the push driver restored because a push session cannot be asked twice).
The share commits under the push construction's own stage word
`phase-formulate` — no new stage vocabulary.

### D6 — The verify question: an independent verify share; fix rounds carry the context

Ruled explicitly: **the formulate share gets an independent verify share;
the settlement itself judges grammars alone.** Grounds, against P3 and the
push path's own review seam: the push path's judgment is two-shaped — the
driver's mechanical collect (grammars, ported to the settlement) and the
wrap-up session judging the charter-as-acceptance over the commit range (a
fresh session) — and the executor's verify kind *is* that wrap-up session
converged: `renderWrapup` over the formulation unit already carries the
ledger (wrapup.ts's own formulate arm), the verify fence's report/gaps
paths fit unchanged, the different-token rule and 0273's settled terminal
compose unchanged, and the close-out (`closeVerified` — the tick and
rename) rides the standing verify-pass close over the same unit. A
settlement judging content would collapse the fresh eyes P3 demands. A
verify FAIL opens the standing fix round over the formulation unit — the
mechanical routing (`fixing`, keyed on the landed verdict) unchanged — and
the fix grant carries the formulation context (D4's arm) so the repair
writes the plan's surfaces; the gap list retires through the standing
`retireGaps` shape and the re-verification closes.

### D7 — The catalog, the wire, and the prompt

`menu.types.formulate` joins the catalog from the same table the fence
reads (one source, two renderings): `writes: { scope: "none", paths:
[the placeholder index, the placeholder pending document, the coverage
map] }`, `never` rendered from the standing sources **minus the family**
— the done half of the unit-state grammar and the phases half of the index
grammar derived from `UNIT_STATE_DOC`'s and `INDEX_DOC`'s own sources. The
letter's honest limit, stated once: another phase's task index matches
neither the formulate never grammar nor the writes anchor — the per-unit
anchor names the one admitted index, and a deny-list provisioner reading
the letter alone under-dennies the rest there; the fence refuses them, and
the coordinator's boundary checks are the authority either way (0261 D3's
posture, the catalog carrying no enforcement claim). `WORK_TYPE_IDS`
grows the id (satisfies-proven); measured, the block serializes to 1910
bytes (the formulate entry 422), under the floor test's 4096 ceiling.
The wire grows in the same change: the request layer's kind vocabulary and
its stop-and-ask message, the worker's `formulate` case (a leased unit
claim like execute, carrying `phase` and `index` beside the standing
fields), the hub's kind set and usage. executor.md's item 2 grows the
enumeration and its own formulate letter — the write family in prose, the
input todo's exclusion, the close-out shapes refused — and the
independence sentence names the formulate producer; person.md is
deliberately untouched (the person surface gains nothing — the person
brackets already ride the standing gates and questions).

### Declined here

The person-phase-id claim address (D1); the doc-lane and worktree tiers
(D2); coordinator-side construction of the formulation todo (the compose
loop, synthesis and plan gate are push-path machinery the protocol's
consumer has not asked to move — P21, and the construction is the person's
bracket); freeform writes for the formulate kind (D4); settlement-judged
content (D6); a verify-carried formulation context (verify's report/gaps
arm is closed by design — the family must never reach it, and the fence's
arm keys on the kind); a `raisedBy`-style new wire field for the grant's
context (the two additive fields are the least the fence and collect
need); and rebuilding the landed-todo digest or the driver's row restore
(D5).
