# 0123 — requirement intake: the category scope (a requirement enters through its family, never the incident's surfaces)

Trigger: the 0120 aftermath, measured after the fact. The reservation ruling followed the 0110
discipline at the ruling level — Derives P3/P5/P13/P19, the tier, the incident census — yet its
enactment enumerated the incident's surfaces alone. The family it actually rules — *the documents a
phase and its tasks produce, and the writer of each* — carried, and still carries at this writing,
same-family teaching the ruling outlaws: the three shipped packs' implement-phase splitting criteria
("each item declares the source files it changes plus its task report" — `intents/cleanroom.md`,
`intents/faithful.md`, `intents/faithful-lean.md`, rendered into every implement phase's planning
session); the zero-intent floor's own `## plan-duties-m` partial ("artifacts are the source-code
changes plus the task reports"); two handover surveys listing `report.md` as an example task artifact
(`templates/prompts/phase-handover.md`, `prompts/run-handover.md`); and the audit duty naming its
per-task findings document in prose while the declared grammar (`Task-artifacts:` — parsed,
driver-owned names refused, its consumer the formulation's seeded obligation rows) sat unused in the
same file's header. None of this is 0120 being wrong about its rule: it is the enactment having no
category boundary to be complete against. And the session that surfaced this repeated the same shape
from the other side — asked why a spec-read task still writes a report, its first move was corpus
archaeology (a grep), which 0110 D5 forbids as a starting point. This ruling fixes the intake — the
way a requirement becomes work — not merely this family's fragments; the family's repair is 0124,
ruled in the same change and processed under this discipline as its first application.

This ruling Derives: **P5** (single ownership — the showing: a requirement family is a mechanism with
owners; an enactment that patches a subset of its homes leaves the family with surfaces teaching
against each other, a split ownership of the rule itself; the category scope is the family's ownership
map made explicit per ruling) and **P19** (measured evidence — the showing: the home-set is enumerated
from the corpus at ruling time, each home cited by path, measured never assumed; the 0120 leftovers
enumerated above are the measured evidence this ruling stands on). It **Amends the
requirements-governance framework** (docs/principles.md's How-to-rule section, 0110 D5/D13 — the
principle restated: *a requirement enters through its family*; the ruling names the family and
enumerates its homes, and the enactment is complete against the enumeration, never against the
incident; provenance carried: 0110 D5's "answered from principles.md, not a corpus search" and D13's
admission and traceability discipline). Tier touched: **governance** — the constitution-layer
amendment re-keys at the next release cut (0110 D14's bracket: a release event is the only moment the
constitution changes); the ruling document and the operative intake procedure (AGENTS.md's
development-conventions layer — this repository's own build rules, outside the tool's constitution)
land now.

## D1 — the Category scope section, a required element of every requirement-bearing ruling

Every plans/NNNN document whose decisions touch mechanism, data, or copy carries a `## Category
scope` section: the requirement family named in one line, then the family's home-set enumerated at
ruling time — every surface that carries, declares, or teaches the family — each home marked one of
*changed by this ruling*, *consistent, untouched (reason)*, or *deliberately deferred (reason and the
deferral's home)*. The section is written before the enactment (it is the measuring step, not the
changelog); the enactment's completeness is judged against it, and an enactment touching a proper
subset of a ruling's own enumeration is an audit finding at the next release. This is 0110 D5 made
operational end to end: the principles answer what the family is, the enumeration measures where it
lives, and the enactment is bounded by the measurement — closing the gap 0120 exposed between a
principled ruling and an incident-enumerated enactment.

## D2 — the intake procedure (operative now, AGENTS.md's layer)

AGENTS.md "Development conventions" gains the operative copy. The procedure in brief:

1. Answer the boundary from docs/principles.md first — a corpus search is never the first move (0110
   D5). A proposal matching no principle is resolved before work begins: fix the set or fix the
   ruling.
2. Name the requirement family; locate its latest ruling and its homes — plans/INDEX.md by principle
   assignment, docs/structure.md by module.
3. Enumerate the home-set (grep is this step's instrument — measuring after the principle answer);
   read the family's latest ruling in full before writing.
4. Write (or extend) the plans/NNNN document with its Category scope section and per-block
   Derives/Amends plus the tier (0110 D5).
5. Enact category-complete — every enumerated home changed or explicitly held, the code home and the
   test home named in the same change (0110 D13); goldens and ratchet pins move with their sources in
   the same change.
6. plans/INDEX.md's row and AGENTS.md's navigation line land in the same change.

## D3 — the release-audit hook

The per-release traceability audit (0110 D13) gains one check: every post-0123 ruling that touches a
family carries its category scope, and no enumerated home teaches against a ruling of the same family
(the 0120-leftover class). Findings land in the release record's disposition. Derives P8's deliberate
enforcement moments: the release event is already the constitution's; the family-consistency
dimension joins the audit that runs there — nothing new is invented between releases.

## D4 — deliberately not done

No tooling or parser enforces the intake: the discipline governs authors and reviewers, and machinery
waits for its consumer (P21 — a second measured incident of category-incomplete enactment would be
that consumer). No retroactive category scopes for pre-0123 rulings (their INDEX rows stand; D3's
check starts at rulings dated after this one). No change to the falsification replay or the audit
cadence. And no attempt to govern a person's asking — the procedure starts at the moment work begins
on a request, in whatever words the request arrived.
