  - D3 — the wave loop's deltas: the pick becomes the ready set topped up to the width (per-task baselines recorded at each launch); the scoped share (the add limited to the ended task's effective scope, never the whole tree); the residue check relaxes to the wave's truth; fix children ride the wave under their task's own effective scope.

  - D5 — parks defer to the drain: the parked handoff stays uncommitted inside its scope (tolerated like a gap list) and the park commit, amendment riding it, lands when no child is in flight; when the blocker closes before the drain the park is skipped altogether.
  - D6 — the declaration discipline (run-plan): a documentation-scoped task also carries a `Touches:` field line and `Depends: none` where its work really is independent; the declarations are what the wave admits on; declaring is harmless in a serial run, so the discipline is unconditional, not gated on the phrase.

  - D8 — concurrent suites share one tree: an anchor that fails on infrastructure-shaped noise while a sibling builds is a relaunch with that feedback, not a stall; the driver's drain callback / strict wait was considered and rejected for the suite.
  - D9 — restart re-derives a wave from disk widened (uncommitted content spanning several task scopes → relaunch each in index order, the wave re-forming under the repeated `parallel:` phrase); deliberately not replicated: the worktree lane family (0068), the landing guard's quarantine (stray writes stop and report — the person's call), any anchor serialization window.
