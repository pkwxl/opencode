## The ruling

### The classifier: the repo's own commit grammar

The class is marked mechanically, by the grammar every commit in these
repositories already carries. The driver has exactly one road into the
tree — the staged, hooked commit — and every coordinator commit stamps
the `Auto-Stage` trailer: shares through `shareMessage` (`exec`, `fix`,
`wrapup`, `park`), the task close-out through its `done` message, lane
landings through `laneLandingMessage` (`landing`), the push path's merge
repair (`merge-repair`) and index ticks (`landing-sync`) beside them. A
**person amendment** is a commit on the walked chain that (a) has exactly
one parent — no merge structure — and (b) carries no driver stage in its
message's trailer block: `driverStage(body) === undefined`, the wall's own
classifier (`sealedChannelCheck`, 0132 D2), reading the trailer block's
grammar (0173 D2: a prose mention of the marker is not a trailer block —
pinned here by test). The person's direct commits — a housekeeping commit
on main, a `.gitignore` edit inside a lane repo — never carry the trailer;
that absence is the class's whole mark.

Everything else on the chain is **terminal divergence, byte-identical to
the pre-change walk**: a commit carrying the driver's stage grammar that
no share booked (a share outside this ledger, a forged trailer —
driver-family shape, never the person's act); a merge commit that is not
a booked landing (the second parent rides history the walk cannot bound);
octopus or malformed links; a chain that does not reach the booked base;
an unreadable history. The boundary is deliberately conservative (P12):
the class admits plain, untrailered, first-parent commits since the
booked base and declines all else.

The boundary's honest letter, stated once: a session that breaks the
standing commit prohibition mints a commit in the person's grammar, and
the walk cannot distinguish it from the person's own act — the wall's
identical accepted trade (0132 D2 ruled the same class boundary for the
sealed channel), the log line below naming every commit the walk passed
so the audit trail stays honest, and the prohibition itself untouched
where it lives (the prompt's standing letter, the fence, P4's close-out
audit). The tool checks grammars, never intent (P7); intent is judged by
the person reading the log and the history.

### Where the class rides: the walk's one home

The walk is `settledChain(at, base, head)` in the coordinator — the one
home both shapes of witness read. It walks the first-parent chain from
`head` down to `base` exactly as `knownHead` did (the booked table: share
commits and close commits as plain commits, landing merges as merges) and
classifies every commit the table does not know: person amendment →
collected and passed; anything else → the walk fails, the caller's
blocked answer byte-identical. `knownHead` becomes its boolean front over
the main tree (the lane-landing gate `settleShare` already reads, the
scoped settlement's booked-parent proof, the worktree admission loop in
`scopedDecision`). The four caller sites that held exact-equality arms —
`preview`'s handed-over share and continuation checks, `verification`'s
producer witness, `fixing`'s gap-list/handover witness — join the same
walk over their own checkout (the lane worktree for worktree entries, the
main tree else): `head === expected` was the same rule with an empty
tolerance set, and the arms' reachable chains hold no booked commits
above their anchors (the entry is its chain's latest), so what the walk
adds there is the amendment class and nothing else — the standing suites
pinning. This is the finding's wedge unwedged on both sides: the lane's
own HEAD past a supervisor commit (`verification`'s arm over the lane
checkout) and main's HEAD past an operator commit (the landing gate and a
fresh worktree admission).

### The log's carrier: the walk's own line

The person-amendment log is a **`log()` line at the walk's exit** — the
precedent's own carrier, `sealedChannelCheck`'s `ℹ` amendment line
mirrored: the count, the short shas, what marks the class ("no driver
stage trailer, the person's direct acts"), and the review command
(`git log --oneline <base>..<head>`). Chosen over the alternatives on the
walk's own surfaces: persisting into the booking or the progress record
would be newly persisted state for what every witness re-derives from
history (P6 refused); a wire field or menu entry would spend frozen
vocabulary on a detection record with no protocol consumer (P13/P21); a
step-detail clause would graft findings onto answers whose shapes are
floors. The line prints where the hosting process prints — in the
daemon's worker, stdout, which `spawnWorker` pipes into the run record
the operator reads; best-effort per P18's letter (journals over a
committed trail — the amendment itself is durable in git history, the
line the finding that names it). It fires only when the walk passed
amendments and succeeded (P8: a finding, never a block; P10: the
exactly-booked chain logs nothing, pinned).

### The re-basing mechanics: nothing persists

The witness re-bases past the amendment by derivation, not by record:
each walk that passes a person commit simply continues down the chain,
and the anchors that matter are booked from the live HEAD at their own
moments — the next grant's `base` is the claim-time HEAD (the person's
commit included), the next settlement's booked `parent` the HEAD it
commits onto, the lane landing's `parent` the main HEAD at
`prepareExecutorLanding` — so the first coordinator act past the
amendment pins above it and every later walk starts above it. Nothing is
written about the amendment itself; restart re-derives the same pass
(the restart-path suites pinning the flows). The verify range rides the
same derivation: a verification granted over an amendment judges the
person's commit with the unit's work — exactly the d17366a8 decision's
own shape, the person's act part of the tree the fresh eyes inspect.

### The prompt arm: the accepted act vocabulary, named where the worker reads it

executor.md's solo-worker item 2 — the paragraph that owns the commit
prohibition — gains the vocabulary sentences: the person's direct commits
(plain commits carrying none of the coordinator's stage trailers) may
appear in the workspace's history between the executor's own shares; the
protocol logs and passes them; never author such a commit yourself — the
prohibition unchanged — and an isolation block over any other unknown
commit still means divergence. The placement is the commit paragraph
because the vocabulary is about commits; the block-meaning clause rides
it so the worker reading a passed amendment does not learn to read
isolation blocks leniently. The ratchet's coupling arm (0203's
both-sides oracle) holds the manual sentences to the walk's anchors —
`settledChain`'s shape, the `driverStage` call, the log line — so
neither side drifts.

### Declined here

Widening the class to merge commits (the second parent rides unbounded
history — structure the walk cannot read; a person needing a merge gets
today's divergence answer, conservatively). Admitting untrailered
commits outside the walked chain — the unbooked settlement's own parent
witness (`HEAD moved outside the booked share`) keeps 0272's escalated
ladder: a person commit inside the report window, after the booking's
base is fixed and before the share commits, cannot be re-based past
without rewriting the booking's commit identity mid-verdict (P4's booked
witness; the ladder already reaches the person, whose remedy there is to
reset — the closed loop 0272 pins). The booked identity proofs —
`landExecutorWorkspace`'s parent/share equalities, `closeVerified`'s
share equality, `nestedWitnesses`' per-root proofs — stay exact (0254,
0249, 0258's letters; they prove a booked operation, not a chain, and
the finding's incidents never touched them). Persisting the amendment
list or a shown-once marker (P6). Carrying the log on the wire, the menu
or a response field (no consumer; the detection line's readers are the
operator and the audit). And touching person.md — the finding's
alternative remedy arm, teaching the person their lane acts: declined
because the enacted arm names the vocabulary where the machine's reader
(the worker) meets it, while the person's own acts need no permission
from a prompt — P2's bracket is already theirs, and the person-side
teaching belongs to the operator's runbook the stand-down record already
carries (the never-commit-directly-in-a-lane rule the site wrote), not to
the protocol's person surface.

## Residuals

- The log line repeats per walk: every witness read that passes the same
  amendments re-logs them (a polling claim re-derives; the line is
  best-effort stdout, bounded by poll frequency). Showing-once would
  persist; the repeat is the honest cost of derivation (P6).
- The equality arms now admit booked commits above their anchors in
  principle, not only amendments. No reachable flow today places a booked
  commit there (the arms' entry is its chain's latest, pinned by the
  standing suites); should a future flow produce one, the walk's rule —
  booked-or-amendment — is the semantics, stated here so the growth is
  not silent.
- The class covers the walked chains alone: a person commit between a
  grant and its report still meets the unbooked settlement's exact-base
  arm (the decline above), so the accepted-act window is *between* the
  executor's shares — the prompt arm's own wording — while *during* a
  share's report window the standing ladder teaches reset. The split is
  the booking's commit identity, stated rather than papered over.
