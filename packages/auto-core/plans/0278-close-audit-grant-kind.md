## The ruling

### D1 — The claim address: the standing unit's own task-shaped id

Protocol claims are unit-addressed; the close-audit is round-scoped — the
design question named and answered: **the address is the standing unit's
own task id** (`claim {"task": "T-002", "kind": "audit"}`). Grounds, the
0276 discipline against the unit-keyed machinery: the durable record the
claim keys on is task-shaped — the index row, the todo, the done rename,
the findings document under `docs/T-NNN/`, the tick — and the standing unit
IS a task in every machinery sense (`run` dispatches it, the wrap-up
verifies it, the close-out renames it); the coordinator's whole machinery
(`latest`, `attempts`, receipts, leases, the close-out) is unit-addressed,
so no second address space is invented (P5/P6). The round-id alternative is
declined with 0276's own grounds plus one more: `R-NN` is the round's own
name — a claim address that names the round would collide with the round's
identity in every path vocabulary (docs/R-NN is a directory, not a unit)
and fork `latest(task)` for a unit that is not the round; the unit↔round
mapping is functional both ways through the marker field the constructor
wrote. 0277's phase-id ruling is distinguished, not contradicted: there the
durable record was phase-shaped because no task existed; here the task
exists by construction — the constructor's whole design is one delta-scoped
task unit. The write surface and the round's identity ride the grant as
data — `findings`, the document path (`closeAuditFindingsDoc`'s own
construction), and `round`, the marker field's own number — derived once at
preview, read by the fence and the collect, never looked up twice (0276's
two-field discipline: the least the arm and the collect need).

### D2 — The lease shape: serial, the main tree

The audit unit declares no `Touches:` (the constructor's own render), so it
is neither documentation-lane eligible nor worktree-lane eligible — and the
terminal phase's every other task is done (the shape the constructor keyed
the unit on), so `scopeCandidates` is empty by its own standing predicate:
**no gate added** (0277's precedent — the formulate slice needed its gate
because a person's `--new-task` row could stand beside the formulation
unit; here a person-added row beside the audit unit is the same work `run`
would dispatch first push-side, and the standing predicate admits exactly
that). The audit grant rides the serial shape: no scope, the main checkout,
the serial reservation holding every other claimant off; the unit's chain
ends at the verify close-out's `closeCommit`, which is terminal in the
standing predicate.

### D3 — The claimable derivation, the brief, and the follow-up route

The audit step derives where the standing surface already recognized the
unit: `deriveExecutorPreview`'s task branch — the marker field
(`task.closeAudit`) deriving the audit step for a unit the plan routes as
next or targeted, pending (never in progress — an in_progress unit belongs
to a push session or its recovery ladder and keeps the standing
specialized-routing wait's own bytes, conservatively waited, P12; a marker
that does not parse falls to the same wait, the honest conservative read).
The construction never happens coordinator-side: with no close-due set, an
audit phase present, or no close attempt run yet, no unit stands and the
surface is byte-identical (P10, pinned) — the unit exists because the
person's close attempt wrote it, exactly the formulate construction's
letter (0276: the construction stays the person's act). The brief is the
same derivation the push-side audit session reads: `renderWhole` over the
standing unit, its todo the single input (the registered template's own
rendering — the due rows, the delta, the findings path, the verdict
grammar), the same facts and views. `MAX_RESPONSE_BYTES` applies through
the standing `withAnswers` bound. The follow-up route (the landed share's
verification or fix round) bypasses the audit branch through the standing
`followUp` flag — the wrap-up and fix sessions claim the unit while its
todo still stands pending, exactly the push path's shape and 0276's own
bypass. The audit kind joins claim's asked-kind guard and the untargeted
claim's take-the-next-step letter unchanged.

### D4 — The landing fence's per-kind permitted arm: the findings document beside the handoff

### D5 — The settlement: the mechanical collect and the no-fix rule

The audit settlement runs the push path's own grammars, through their own
functions — `parseResult` (the result line, the one `Result:` grammar home:
the document must end with `Result: PASS` or `Result: FAIL <one-sentence
reason>`, the receipt line the driver reads) and the due-row linkage
(`gatherAdjudications`' own naming rule, exported from its one home: every
row the round owes at the closing tree — the cover set read with the unit's
own findings document excluded from the adjudication gather, so the
claim-time set the constructor keyed the unit on stands — named in the
document). Read-only, ahead of any staging and past the zero-write answer
(a share that wrote nothing has no output to judge — 0277's placement, the
formulate precedent's zero-write floor), replayable; its problems are the
push grammar's own vocabulary, the holder's remedy a rewrite of its own
document — the plain blocked answer by 0272's classification, never
escalated. A table that does not parse at settlement stands the naming
check down (the constructor's own posture — it keys nothing on an
unjudgeable set; the close gate holds the named problem separately, the
person's moment). **The no-fix rule (the program's settled ruling, carried
and pinned): the audit kind lands its findings whatever the verdict — a
FAIL never opens any grant.** Grounds, P2 and the push path's own shape:
the FAIL finding is the person's decision surface (the red path's letter:
"a FAIL is a finding for the person, routed to them at the round's close,
and the remedy it names is theirs to order, never yours to enact"); it
surfaces through the round-close machinery exactly as today (the cover-set
gate naming the standing unit's findings document — "the close-audit unit
T-NNN already ran … a FAIL is a finding for the person, never repaired
in-round"); and structurally the audit share's landed receipt carries no
verdict — the fail→fix funnel (`fixing`, keyed on a landed verify
verdict) can never fire off an audit share. The funnel is the verify
path's own alone: a verify share's own FAIL over the audit unit opens the
standing fix round repairing the document's acceptance (0276's identical
shape over the formulation unit), never enacting the finding's remedy.
Three push halves deliberately not rebuilt on the protocol (P21 — no
window): the close arms' construction (the person's close attempt), the
baseline citation (`updateAssuranceBaselines`, the close arms' own commit),
and the person's remediation itself. The share commits under the push
family's own stage word `close-audit` — the construction commits' and the

close-audit-specific stage the push vocabulary carries; the session's own
work lands push-side under the ordinary task execute stage, so the
family's stamped word is the one reused (no new stage vocabulary), keeping
the landed share's provenance distinguishable from an ordinary execute
share's — the FAIL finding's reader reads the audit's commits by their
stage.

### D6 — The verify question: an independent verify share; the no-fix bound holds

Ruled explicitly, against P3 and the push path's own shape, both
precedents cited: **the audit share gets an independent verify share; the
settlement itself judges grammars alone; and the verify share's own FAIL is
the only funnel that ever opens.** Grounds, 0276's met: the push path's
review seam for the close-audit unit is **two-shaped** — the driver's
mechanical reading (the adjudication grammar, ported to the settlement)
and the wrap-up session judging the acceptance over the commit range (a
fresh session — `runCloseout` runs `runWrapup` for the unit like every
task; the red path's own proof: the unit completes with its report passing
while the findings' FAIL routes to the person) — and the executor's verify
kind *is* that wrap-up session converged: `renderWrapup` over the audit
unit is task-shaped and exists, the verify fence's report/gaps paths fit
unchanged, the different-token rule and 0273's settled terminal compose
unchanged, and the close-out (`closeVerified` — the tick and rename) rides
the standing verify-pass close over the same unit. 0277's decline grounds
are not met here and are not borrowed: the audit unit is not a one-shot
bypass session (it runs the ordinary closeout, wrap-up included), and the
wrap-up brief exists — a settled-not-verified shape would drop the fresh
eyes P3 demands where the push path runs them. A verify FAIL opens the
standing fix round over the audit unit carrying the findings context (D4),
the gap list retiring through the standing `retireGaps` shape, the
re-verification closing — the document repaired, never the finding
enacted. The findings' verdict itself (PASS or FAIL) is inert to the
routing: a compliant FAIL document passes the acceptance (the template's
own grammar allows both), the close-out completes, and the FAIL surfaces
at the person's close attempt — the no-fix rule's composition with the
verify chain.
