### S2 failure scenarios

- **S23-01:** A person installs a same-named `decompose-m.md` containing only the three guarded strings. Startup accepts it. The session receives no `Artifacts:` teaching, so its checklist can produce no declaration specs and the later per-subtask declared-artifact checks do not run.
- **S23-02:** A `whole.md`, `subtask.md` or `fix.md` overlay loads without the handover grammar. When the driver reaches a handover wall, `handoverVerdict` sees no valid status, enters rollback/retry, and blocks after the second invalid attempt; a `handoff-steer` overlay can similarly omit the path while passing on its two status strings.
- **S23-03:** A pack writes `whloe:` instead of `whole:` or misspells its phase qualifier. `guaranteesProblem` returns clean, no render matches the assertion, and a later prompt can lose the supposedly guaranteed text without any error.
- **S23-05:** A project customizes the prompt library but the phase-charter lead-in, side-effect instructions, prerequisite warning and formulation protocol still come from source literals. Its template review/override does not cover the actual session-facing copy; 0095's former `phase-plan.md` overlay is explicitly ineffective.

## Design notes

The embedded registries and strict surface parsers are healthy; the scoped tests are fast and green. The weakest choice is the hand-maintained three-way parser/template/marker correspondence: it has already drifted, and one test blesses an incomplete decomposition override. Fix that census and unknown assert targets first. The largest architectural tension is 0095's consciously code-built formulation document versus P15's renderer-only rule; it needs a maintainer ruling before a move. Treat raw marker validation as an accident guard for trusted project configuration, not as a sandbox.
