## Design notes

- Health: a careful, heavily-commented store with sound single-process crash discipline (atomic writes, depreciation, clamps) and good unit coverage of each API in isolation. Its weak axis is **identity across time**: what a figure belongs to is decided by "the bucket currently open", which was right for 0019's serial single-session world and is wrong at every later seam — doc-lane siblings (S15-03), the phase boundary (S15-06), the round boundary (S15-02), the crash window (S15-01), and the resumed session (S15-04).
- Best choice: the lenient parse + "stats is never a source of truth" posture — no stats defect can affect flow or exit codes (every write swallowed, every reader guarded).
- Worst choice: the task stint closes at the *next* task's start (0152 D2 rode the existing destruction seam). It made the ledger cheap but inherits every boundary's time and labels; the ledger is the one accounting surface meant to be compared across forks, so it needs stint-own identity.
- Schema: `v` is never checked and unknown fields are dropped on the first write (an older binary strips a newer section) — acceptable under the never-a-source posture, but worth one sentence in 0019's successor if a v:2 ever lands.
- Change first: S15-06 + S15-01 together (one ruling amending 0152 D2: stint-own phase/round, a load-time ledger-tail check), then S15-03/S15-02 (two small store fixes), then S15-04 (the emitter's delta).
