## The ruling

**The confined observation.** The settlement's `changes` gains the grant's
own per-kind artifact paths (`report`/`gaps` on a verify, the `handoff`
otherwise) when two conditions hold: the plain porcelain rows do not
already carry the path, and a confined `--ignored` read of exactly those
literal paths answers `!!`. The read is one `git status --porcelain -z
--no-renames -uall --ignored -- <literals>` against the checkout; its rows
enter `changes` with git's own `!!` status letter. No other path is ever
observed ignore-aware — the fence is unchanged, 0249's letters hold for
every non-artifact surface, and the sibling-scope carve of
`reportedChanges` composes unchanged ahead of the augmentation (an artifact
the plain rows carry but a sibling's scope claims is *seen* and not
re-added, exactly as today).

**The force-staged landing.** The per-root staging partitions on the status
letter: plain rows stage under `add -A` exactly as before; `!!` rows —
which by the confinement above can only be the per-kind artifact paths —
stage under `add -f`, because git refuses an ignored pathspec without it.
From there every proof is the standing machinery unchanged: the write-tree,
the diff-tree staged-set proof (the shadowed artifact among `observed`,
permitted by the fence's per-kind branch), the booking's tree witness, the
hooked commit under the share's message, the replay. Once landed the
artifact is tracked; the fix branch's `ls-tree` guard finds its input, the
close-out retires the gap list through the ordinary tracked-file
deletion, and the entry is inert for the path from then on.

**The decline.** The serve/registration lint the finding's remedy family
named is declined (P21): with the settlement tolerating the shadow, a
shadowing rule changes no protocol behavior — after the first landing the
path is tracked and ignore no longer applies to it — so a warning surface
would name nothing the person needs to act on. The one-line cleanup
(removing the stale entry) remains the person's own option; the field
already taught it through the question the verify child raised
(`d17366a8`), whose decision this ruling makes non-blocking either way.
