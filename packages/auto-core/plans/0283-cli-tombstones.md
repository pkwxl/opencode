## The standing dispositions

- **The task-append session** (`src/loop-append.ts`) — removed from the
  pull path: planPrelude refuses `append` at its own door (the daemon's
  plan operation refuses the request field with the same teaching), and
  appends ride task-add (one task at a time, no session — the tasks
  operation) and re-formulation (a changed planning input re-formulates
  the plan through its formulation). The loop's internal append arms
  (loop-append, the m-mode implied append of an input on a non-empty
  index) stand for the demolition track.
- **The repair-append budget** (`src/loop-task.ts`'s repairRoundAppend,
  the arms in runSerialUnit, the doc loop's finishBlocked and
  loop-phase's regression/verdict gates) — removed: a FAIL verdict
  surfaces as state, the block's question carrying its own remedy; the
  `repair` run option and the blocked outcome's `repair` fact retire
  with it; the wrapup ladder's comments re-keyed. The executor's fix
  grant repairs verify FAILs on the pull path; the coordinator's
  `fixBudget` blocked reason is its own bound, untouched.
- **The charter verifier** (`verifyPlanStep`, `verifyPlanPrompt`, the
  planning input's admission warn, the handover's write-time check) —
  off: the plan and append steps are deterministic (no verifier session
  ahead of them, no blockage diagnosis over a verdict), the
  OPENCODE_AUTO_PLAN_VERIFY switch a retired-variable tombstone, and
  prompt-verify keeps its mechanical halves (the clamp's check call, the
  audit append, the reply parsers). The guarantees' mechanical asserts
  and the synthesize settlement's checks (0281) judge what the sessions
  compose.
- **The knowledge/prior-knowledge extraction** (`src/knowledge.ts`'s two
  session functions, their templates and renderers, the k phase's
  extraction arm, the preflight dispatch role) — retired (0277's
  residual confirmed): a task-less phase runs no session (its route goes
  straight to the boundary stop and the handover), the read halves stay
  (the digests the formulation assembly reads over committed documents),
  and the start gate's leftover teaching names the retired extraction's
  interrupted write as the person's delete.
- **The push lane workers** (`src/shell.ts`'s defaultLaneLauncher and
  selfInvoke) — removed: no shell registers a launcher, the lanes
  dispatch refuses loudly without one, and the server profile's lane
  override (which spawned payloads the worker already refuses) is
  deleted. The `laneLauncher` seam stays for a host that owns worker
  processes; the scheduler itself is the demolition track's.
- **person.md** — retired with 0282 (its letter honored early; recorded
  here as the docket's own line).
