# 0244 — The assurance table and the cover-set gate: delta-scoped verification

Status: **active-authority — accepted by the maintainer (2026-11-16);
enactment staged S1–S6 below, each stage always-green, the person commits
at boundaries** — three coordinated pieces over one measured failure shape:
**A**, the **assurance table** (`docs/assurance.md`, target-declared data —
the capability catalog's twin applied to assurance itself: standing invariant
rows with watched surfaces, kinds, cadences, close behaviors and cited
baselines); **B**, **receipts with validity** (every verification receipt
gains a tree range and the paths it decides — a verdict becomes valid over a
range, invalidated exactly by the surfaces it intersects); **C**, the
**due-set computation and the cover-set gate** (the driver intersects the
round's measured delta with each row's watched surface; at round close the
cover set must be valid — mechanical rows block on fresh receipts, judgment
rows accrue or are `close-due` and adjudicated by the round's last task
unit, the **close-audit**). The admission evidence is the cleanroom baseline
R-07 census (2026-11-15..16, measured first-hand): a triage round whose
entire deliverable-tree delta was **one test file** (72+/9− across two
commits in the isolated repo) paid the full four-phase shell — a six-task
test phase, a thirteen-task audit phase whose eight standing dimensions each
re-derived the whole substrate (~450 lines per findings document, the same
eight R-06 paid for a full repair round), six full-suite walks (the pin pair,
the repaired pair, the audit's re-run pair after a one-file correction moved
the tree) — and then **stopped two tasks short of its own verdict** (no
`audit.md`/`verdict.md`, `## Close` unfilled, no run active): completion is
judged by task ticks and nothing mechanical requires the assurance to be
whole. The round also exhibits the two companion diseases the design
carries remedies for: the charter drift (the person's outcome — fix the
five — silently narrowed at drafting into the external receipt's
classification framing, no reconciliation surfaced at the analysis gate) and
the instrument asymmetry (the deciding evidence lived on an instrument the
round could not lawfully run; its own reopen fork was structurally
guaranteed to fire, and the fix-first charter that resolved it a day later
was invented outside the round system).

## The measured findings

1. **The assurance cost is round-invariant, not delta-proportional.** R-06
   (a full repair round) and R-07 (delta: one test file) paid the *same*
   eight standing audit dimensions (R-06 T-258..T-265, R-07 T-289..T-296),
   each dimension's findings re-deriving the whole substrate from zero. The
   audit's splitting key is the standing surface (the bundle duty's
   dimension list; the faithful packs' "one item per baseline behavior
   family"), never the round's delta — the mandate is unconditional in all
   three current bundles.
2. **The formulation session receives no measured delta.** The formulation
   todo's inputs are prose (brief, round brief, analysis, handovers, the
   prior-round conclusions excerpt); nothing carries the round's commit
   range or changed paths, and the survey duty reads *state*, not *change*.
   A planner told to be proportionate has nothing to be proportionate to.
3. **Tree-currency coupling double-charges every motion.** R-07's T-284 — a
   one-file correction after the test phase's walks — moved the closing
   tree, and the audit's "re-run every deciding check first-hand" then
   re-ran the full suite pair again: six full-suite walks total for one
   margin repair. No receipt carries a validity range, so no later phase
   can cite an earlier phase's evidence lawfully.
4. **Nothing requires the assurance whole.** R-07 stopped after T-296 with
   the consolidation (T-298) and the catalog extension (T-297) unexecuted —
   no verdict document, `## Close` unfilled, no run active. Completion is
   judged by task ticks; a round can simply run out with its assurance
   unconcluded, and no check notices.
5. **Charter drift is invisible at the gate.** The person's instruction
   (fix the five) and the drafted round charter (classify; route code
   defects to a successor) diverged at drafting; the analysis gate released
   a roadmap line whose goal was the external receipt's §4 framing, with no
   reconciliation row surfacing the narrowing for the person's yes.
6. **Instrument asymmetry routes evidence outside the system.** The
   deciding evidence for R-07's five rows lived on the FL test bed's
   instrument; the cleanroom's own lanes were green ×6 on byte-identical
   content, the reopen fork fired by construction, and the resolution —
   the 2026-11-16 fix-first charter, the bed fixing and crossing recipes —
   was a person-invented channel outside the round machinery. The pattern
   (reds on published content, decided by the instrument that can see them)
   is recurring in this family.

## Decisions

### D1 — the delta block (findings 2, 5)

The driver measures the round's delta and renders it into every planning
input: the commit range (the round's opening commit — the G1/establishment
stamp — through the current tree, per deliverable tree, isolated repos
included), the grouped stat (code paths, test paths, docs), and the named
changed tests/files. Pure P7 grammar over git; the anchor is
driver-derivable (the establishment's own commit) and re-derivable on every
read (P6). The floor: no prior commit or empty diff renders nothing — the
todo byte-identical (P10). The block feeds the formulation todo, the append
input, and the due-set computation of D4; it is also the round-brief
drafter's standing input (D6). The block is prompt-side only: the driver
measures, never judges.

### D2 — the assurance table (findings 1, 4)

`docs/assurance.md`, target-declared committed data, the catalog's twin
grammar: one row per standing invariant —

```
| id  | invariant              | kind        | watched surface            | cadence                  | close      | baseline                     |
|-----|------------------------|-------------|----------------------------|--------------------------|------------|------------------------------|
| S-03| MUST coverage          | judgment    | catalog Required rows' paths| on-reach                | accrue     | R-06 audit @<sha> (T-258/*)  |
| S-06| suite green at close   | mechanical  | lane table rows            | every-round              | blocks     | receipts T-287/*            |
| S-08| clean-room independence| judgment    | crossing events, similarity flags | every-round       | close-due  | R-07 audit @<sha>           |
```

The watched surface is paths for coverage/interface/hygiene rows and
process facts for independence rows — declared once per project, seeded by
the pack (the cleanroom seeds its eight; the faithful packs seed their
parity dimensions, nearly free: the behavior baseline the analysis phase
already itemizes is the watched surface), born for an existing project by
transcribing its last audit's conclusions into rows (the T-266 precedent —
append-only evidence cells — applied to assurance itself). The table is the
single owner of *what must hold*; the baselines are cited commits, so the
trail is git-addressable end to end (P18). The core knows the grammar, not
any row's meaning (P16); a future intent participates by seeding rows, and
a project with no table keeps today's behavior exactly (P10).

### D3 — receipts with validity (finding 3)

Every verification receipt gains two fields beside its instrument stamp:
`Tree:` (the base..head range it was produced at) and `Decides:` (the paths
whose verdicts it carries). A verdict is thereby valid over a range and
invalidated exactly by intersection: a later tree motion re-verifies only
what its changed paths intersect, citing the earlier same-shape receipts
for the rest — the disjointness argument is path sets, mechanical,
recordable (P7). The lane contract's receipts (0096/0098) grow the fields
additively; existing receipts read unchanged.

### D4 — the due set and the cover-set gate (findings 1, 4)

Two computations over the table, both driver-side:

- **The due set** — Δ ∩ watched-surface ≠ ∅, plus the cadence rows
  (`every-round` always; `person-forced` and periodic when their condition
  stands). Rendered into the test/audit formulation inputs as the phase's
  owed set (the planner plans exactly it), and recomputed at close.
- **The cover set** — at round close (both arms: the phased close and
  0243's open close), for every due row: a fresh receipt at the closing
  tree (mechanical rows), an adjudication receipt (judgment rows), or a
  valid cited baseline with a recorded disjointness argument. Mechanical
  rows and close-due rows **block** the close (exit 2, the rows named, the
  remedy taught — D5's unit or the audit phase's findings, per the round's
  shape); `accrue` rows ride with the accrual recorded in the close
  listing, surfaced to the person (P2) and paid by the next round that
  reaches them. Green cover set ⇒ the close lands.

This is the finding-4 remedy: a round can no longer run out with its
assurance unconcluded — the close *is* the cover set's validity, as P4 made
committing the completion condition for work.

### D5 — the close-audit unit (findings 1, 4; 0243 composition)

A round whose close-due rows lack adjudication receipts and which carries
no audit phase to produce them (an open round, a trimmed pipeline) gets the
round's **last task unit**: the driver constructs it by the
`constructFormulation` pattern (`src/close-audit.ts`) — one delta-scoped
adjudication task in the round's terminal phase, dispatched through the
ordinary session pipeline (a fresh session — P3's independence was always
about who judges, never which phase label they sat under), judging exactly
the close-due rows at the closing tree, writing `findings.md` and the
`Result:` verdict per the audit artifact grammar, wrap-up verified,
committed. Red or findings ⇒ exit 2, findings routed to the person — never
an in-round repair. Green ⇒ the receipt updates the row's baseline (the
table stays the single memory) and the close proceeds. The unit scales with
the due set: a quiet fix round owes one small session; a recipe-absorbing
round owes its independence and touched rows — never the standing shell.

### D6 — the ask-vs-charter reconciliation (finding 5)

The round-brief proposal channel gains one required block whenever the
drafted goal narrows the person's stated outcome: the person's ask restated
beside the drafted charter, the difference named (what this round will not
do that the ask implied, and where that work goes instead), released with
the proposal for the person's yes. The drafting session's authority to
restate an external document's framing as the round's goal ends where the
ask's outcome begins — the narrowing becomes a decision the person sees
(P2), not a sentence that rides inside the drafted line.

### D7 — the delta-keyed duty copy (finding 1)

The bundle mandates re-key from the standing surface to the due set: the
cleanroom audit duty plans one audit task per compliance dimension **the
due set reaches**, carried dimensions adjudicated in the closing task
against cited baselines with the carry argument recorded — every dimension
named in the consolidation either way; the faithful packs' test/acceptance
duties split by the delta's changed families, the standing baseline suite
running **once per tree state** as the regression bound, determinism ×2 on
changed sections; the builtin `plan-duties-t`/`-v` partials take the same
phrasing shaped to render byte-identically when no table stands. The mode
files stay prose and gain nothing (0243 A; P5 — the due set owns owed
verification; rhetoric cannot buy or waive evidence).

### D8 — the lockstep vocabulary (P13)

One change, enumerated: the table grammar (id, invariant, kind
`judgment|mechanical`, watched surface, cadence
`on-reach|every-round|person-forced|periodic`, close
`blocks|accrue|close-due`, baseline); the receipt fields (`Tree:`,
`Decides:`); the close refusal's wording family (the named-rows exit-2
teaching); the close listing's accrual row. Retirements, when they ever
come, go through tombstones.

### C — declined and deferred (P21)

**Declined:** a blanket close-audit phase for open rounds — the coarse form
of D5; it would reintroduce the standing shell at the close boundary (every
`pr` round paying a full audit regardless of delta). **Declined:** runtime
lane selection by the driver (the gate picks *whether* evidence is owed;
which lane produces it stays the pack's declaration and the planner's
plan — the driver never interprets intent, P15). **Deferred:** the
fix-recipe standing crossing class (finding 6's structural half) — it
amends the migration family's §13 surface, which lives in the target
projects' own principles documents, not this corpus; recorded here as the
identified pattern with its first consumer (the 2026-11-16 charter) named.
**Deferred:** core-side charters for the consolidation totality — the
generic formulation charter and the obligation ledger (0097) already judge
"every due row covered or deferred" once D1/D4 render the due set into the
todo; a pack-specific charter waits for a measured need.

## Residuals

- A watched surface declared too narrowly is a silent hole — the mitigation
  is the `person-forced` and `periodic` cadences (any row re-derivable on
  demand or on schedule), and the declaration discipline is the table's
  load-bearing data work, owned pack-side.
- The judgment/mechanical split is itself a declaration; a misfiled row
  either over-blocks (a mechanical row that needed judgment) or under-blocks
  (the reverse) — surfaced at the first close that reads it, amendable as
  data.
- The blocking gate lands staged (S4 below) — until then the cover set
  renders as a close-listing observation, and the table's baselines need
  one round of receipts before blocking is honest.
- The delta block's anchor is the establishment commit; a round established
  before this change's enactment has no stamped anchor — the first
  enactment-round delta falls back to the prior round's close listing,
  named in the block's rendering.
