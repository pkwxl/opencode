# 0137 — the phases key internal, m mode the compatibility floor, the roadmap line the one reshape channel

The maintainer's round over the supersession note's field reports (2026-10-27, following 0135 and 0136):
after the framing fix and the divergence-only predicate, the standing question was the key itself — the
maintainer's words: "perhaps making phases in config.json internal and no longer exposing it externally,
much like ANALYSIS off, would be a more appropriate choice", and on the m-mode objection: "m mode is
never the case, it can be internal too… aseo and rustpg are just internal works." The census measured
the claim: every real project on the machine is phased (the aster-mig fleet's nine, plus the re-work
bundles' stamps); the only `phases: "m"` project is `rustpg`, an internal work; the tool's own reliance
on m mode is the suite's floor (40 test files, 6 e2e uses as the simplest shape) and the README's
documented contract. With m mode reclassified as floor machinery rather than a person-facing way of
working, the key has **no person-facing consumer left**: the roadmap line can cover any round (an
uncovered round becomes covered the moment its line exists), the `=off` recovery floor reads the key as
machinery (0134 D1's classification already placing it outside the supported world), init needs the key
only as its own birth stamp, and the survey stop / `amend --phases` refusal already taught the line as
the channel at the act's moments.

The analysis: the two-channel precedence (covered rounds reshape by the line, config-governed rounds by
the key — 0114 D3's authority branch) was the last thing keeping the key a person surface, and it was
exactly the confusion source of both field reports: two homes for one fact, the person asked to know
which one wins. One channel removes the question instead of answering it. The machinery needs no
change: `applyTailDrift`/`resolveRoundPhases` already resolve line-??-config internally (the D5
resolution), so the reshape path through the line works for covered and uncovered rounds alike, and the
config key simply never changes after init. `amend --phases` is fully replaceable — the line does
everything it did (the mid-phase boundary scoping survives as the advance's own nothing-to-advance
refusal; the coverage refusal is absorbed into the blanket retirement) — and retiring it is P13's
tombstone lifecycle over a flag, never over the key: **no migration**. Every live config carries a legal
`phases` key; init and the bundles keep stamping it; `fix` never touches it; `rustpg` keeps running
unchanged on the floor.

This ruling Derives: **P21** (machinery waits for its consumer — inverted: a channel retires when its
consumer ceases; the maintainer's field census is the measurement, and after 0136 the key's last
person-facing consumer is the reshape channel this ruling deletes), **P13** (frozen additive-only
vocabularies — the flag tombstoned with the `--amend`/`init --brief` precedent of parse-time retirement
notices, the key never tombstoned, every teaching surface re-pointed in one lockstep), **P8** (the
surfaced lines teach the one channel that exists — the reshape stops, the primer, the analysis stub and
problem texts, the amend refusal all name the line), and **P19** (the field census above; the internal
works named). Tier touched: **CLI surface + teaching copy** — one flag retired, one summary segment
dropped, one guard pruned; no establishment, gate or sync machinery moves.

## The ruling

**D1 — `amend --phases` retires.** The flag is a tombstone on amend alone (init keeps it: the birth
choice), refused at the flag's parse site before value validation — a retired flag's value is never
judged — with the notice naming the one channel: a round's phases reshape by moving or adding its
`- R-NN <phases> — <goal>` line in `docs/analysis.md` (an uncovered round becoming covered the moment
its line exists, the next transfer re-deriving the unstarted tail). `amendPhasesHold` and both its
refusals (0101 D6's mid-phase boundary scoping, 0114 D3's coverage refusal) are pruned: the mid-phase
guard's promise survives as the advance's own nothing-to-advance refusal, the coverage refusal as the
blanket retirement. m mode's `phases` is fixed at birth; the notice's parenthetical says so.

**D2 — the key is init-stamped machinery.** The config `phases` key stays a constitutional key — init
and the `--intent` bundles stamp it, the loader validates it, the D5 fallback reads it for rounds no
released line covers — but it stops being a person surface: `amend` no longer revises it (D1), and
`formatProjectConfig` drops the phases segment (one home: run's banner, `status`, the init/amend
summaries — the round's operative list is the phase tree those surfaces already render). No tombstone
on the key, no migration, `fix` untouched.

**D3 — m mode is the compatibility floor.** The single-phase mode keeps its machinery whole (the
`rustpg` project of the census runs unchanged; the suite's floor keeps its shape; bare `init` still
lands there) and is classified in the README's config row with 0134 D1's sentence pattern: machinery
kept, not a supported way of working for new projects — a phased project starts at `init --phases` or
`--intent`. The `=off` analogy holds exactly: the floor is maintained policy, never a recommended
start.

**D4 — the teaching says one channel.** The reshape stops (`plan`'s held-survey line, `status`'s
awaiting note), the primer (`DRIVER_PRIMER`'s authority sentence), the analysis stub hint and the
roadmap problem text all name the line alone — "move or add its line" — with the key named as
init-stamped machinery, never as a channel. The anti-amend tails ("never by amending the key") retire
with the channel they warned against.

**D5 — the residuals recorded.** The `phases` array form and the bundle manifest stamps are untouched
(D2's machinery); the D5 fallback (a released roadmap planning no line at all keeps the key governing
every round) stands — such a project reshapes by writing its first line; and the retirement's one lost
convenience (one command vs one line edit) is the price of one channel, accepted. A future
re-open would need a measured incident the line channel cannot serve.
